# Original mode icons

These are the original assets requested by the user, copied from MCTiers and SubTiers. The editable SVG/original PNG files are retained unchanged. Minecraft sprites are PNG exports with transparent padding, resized proportionally to the established nameplate and Tab dimensions. No icons are redrawn or recoloured.

Rights to these third-party assets remain with their respective owners. The project license does not grant rights to them.
